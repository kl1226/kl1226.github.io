A CLEARER VIEW

Keep the next thing in sight.

Search your plans, add notes, reschedule, snooze, and mark work done. Choose Chrome popup reminders, system banners, or both.

Chrome must be running for reminders. System banners follow your device’s notification settings.

OPTIONAL GOOGLE CALENDAR

Your schedule,
together.

Connect Google Calendar to see upcoming events from your selected calendars alongside your local work. While connected, new Schedules are also added to your primary calendar, including repeating schedules.

Deadlines stay local. Existing Google events remain managed in Google Calendar. Additional NextDue alerts for Calendar events are off by default.

THOUGHTFUL BY DEFAULT

Quick Add happens on your device.

Your text is parsed locally. NextDue has no developer-operated server, advertising, or analytics. Local items stay in this Chrome profile; optional Calendar features exchange data directly with Google.
